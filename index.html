How RisePact works

  • Each night, set tomorrow's wake time and put 5, 15 or 25 Pact Credits on the line.
  • Thirty minutes before the alarm the pact locks and can no longer be cancelled.
  • When the alarm rings, tap Get Up and walk 20 steps within five minutes. Keep the pact and your credits come back. Snooze, stop or miss the window and they burn.
  • RisePact requires iOS 26 or later and an iPhone that supports step counting. Alarm and Motion & Fitness access are required.

Pact Credits

  • New users receive 5 free credits once. More credits are sold as in-app purchases through the App Store.
  • Credits are a digital item used only inside RisePact. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn or transferred, and are never paid out to anyone.
  • Credits are stored in this iPhone's Keychain. They are not synced between devices.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunded credits are removed from your balance.

Troubleshooting

The alarm will not stop

Open RisePact → Settings → Alarm problem → stop all RisePact alarms. If sound continues, turn off RisePact's alarm access in iPhone Settings. After the lock, stopping this way breaks the pact; sound can always be stopped.

Steps are not counting

Check that Motion & Fitness is allowed for RisePact in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security, carry your iPhone while walking and keep RisePact open. Only new steps after Get Up count; Apple Watch steps are not read. One genuine sensor failure every 30 days is excused and your credits return.

The evening reminder did not arrive

Turn the reminder on in Settings and check notification and Focus settings. The reminder is skipped when tomorrow's pact is already armed.

I reinstalled the app

Your credit balance lives in the Keychain and survives a reinstall. A pact that was locked when the app was deleted counts as broken.

Delete your data

RisePact has no account and no server-side copy of your pacts. Remove RisePact alarms from Settings → Alarm problem, then delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

RisePact Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Türkçe

SupportContact

RisePact is an iPhone alarm app made by pairbeam ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact for privacy questions: berkay@pairbeam.app.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your alarms, pacts, step checks, history and credit balance stay on your iPhone.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card details.
  • RisePact may send anonymous usage signals that do not identify you (see "Anonymous analytics").

Information stored on your iPhone

  • Pacts and settings: wake times, stakes, pact results, streaks, reliability, evening reminder preferences and up to 500 detailed results. Stored in the app's local storage.
  • Pact Credits: your balance, credits on the line and App Store transaction identifiers, stored in this iPhone's Keychain so a reinstall does not reset them. They are not synced to other devices.
  • Widgets: a small summary (next alarm, credits on the line, streak) shared with RisePact's widgets on the same iPhone.
  • Older results: results from earlier RisePact features (Move and Focus) remain on your iPhone and are only shown in history.

We do not operate a server that stores this information, and we cannot see, retrieve or delete it remotely.

Permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to schedule the alarms you set.
  • Motion & Fitness: to count the 20 steps of your wake-up challenge. Step counts are read on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. RisePact does not use HealthKit or read Apple Watch data.
  • Notifications (optional): for the evening reminder and results. These are local notifications; no push server is used.

You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Pact Credits are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. RisePact checks Apple's signed transaction on your iPhone to add the credits once, and removes them if Apple reports a refund. We do not receive your payment details.

Anonymous analytics

To learn whether RisePact works, the app may send anonymous product signals through TelemetryDeck, such as "pact armed", "pact kept or broken", the stake size, "credits purchased" with the pack size, and "onboarding completed", together with basic technical details like app and iOS version. These signals use an anonymised identifier, contain no name, email, location, wake times or step data, are not linked to you and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. If you choose to share alarm diagnostics from the app, the iOS share sheet sends them only to the recipient you pick; they can include app and iOS versions, alarm identifiers and schedules. If you email us, we use your message and attachments only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. To stop RisePact's scheduled activity, remove its alarms in Settings → Alarm problem and turn off the evening reminder, then delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage; the credit balance in the Keychain may remain on the device so that a reinstall cannot reset a locked pact. iPhone backups you create are managed by you and Apple.

Children

RisePact is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your app data, most requests can be handled on your iPhone as described above. For any privacy request or question, including under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK, email berkay@pairbeam.app.

Changes

We will update this page when RisePact or our practices change and revise the effective date above.
